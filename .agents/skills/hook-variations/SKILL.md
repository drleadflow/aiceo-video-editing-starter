# Hook Variations

Keep the body edit and voice mix fixed. Propose 3 or 4 truthful title/visual openings and identify what each tests. Use presets/two-line-impact-red.json when red is requested; retain exactly two short lines and mobile safe margins. Put the hook on frame zero. In a presenter cutout layout, keep the presenter small enough to reveal the B-roll subject, without stretching either layer. A generated clip is not proof of a factual claim.
Create v02, v03 etc with npm run new-video -- <slug> --from projects/<slug>/v01. Inspect faces, captions and audio at each changed boundary. Label variants descriptively and export separately. Do not claim trial-reel publication support just because export succeeded.
