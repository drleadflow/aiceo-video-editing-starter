# Included teaching resources
Selected cut-silences, cut-mistakes, video-storytelling, hyperframes and gsap skills,
EDL review, beat validation and ElevenLabs transcription helpers are adapted from
https://github.com/nateherkai/hyperframes-student-kit at b1afdb1dcbcad39dd27638ea699f132fe44ce6df.
Private project paths were normalized to projects/. Licenses are retained in licenses/.
No AIS branding, personal recordings, third-party songs or private account settings are included.
The optional provider skills and starter onboarding are original integration guidance;
they do not redistribute vendor plugins or confer service access.

Adaptations also remove unavailable producer-only QA scripts and clarify project-specific
QA examples; the starter uses its pinned CLI checks instead.
